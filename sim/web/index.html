These are the four DSP settings at the top of firmware3/main/audio.c. VOX is the gate's open level: a sustained loudness of 22 means about 22% of full scale. The browser mic isn't calibrated like the ES8311's 18 dB gain, so set VOX on the device last.

The board reads these from its power chip (AXP2101) and motion sensor (QMI8658, which also measures board temperature). Here you set them by hand. The battery bar turns yellow at 30 % and red at 15 %. Temp shows on the top readout line as Aurebesh TEMP + the number. Like the device, the console reads temperature every 30 s and battery every 10 s, so slider changes take a moment to show (opening the Sensors page reads them at once).
